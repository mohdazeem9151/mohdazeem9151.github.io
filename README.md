# Mohd Azeem - Research Portfolio

**Live site: [mohdazeem9151.github.io](https://mohdazeem9151.github.io/)**

Mohd Azeem is a social science researcher (M.Sc. Digital Society, Central University of Rajasthan) who works where field research, survey data and code meet. He studies how technology, work and public policy shape lives in India - and builds research tools, including the StockItihas NSE history dashboard.

## What is on the site

- **About** - research profile and interests: labour and livelihoods, digital society, agriculture and rural finance, consumption and inequality
- **Education and training** - M.Sc. Digital Society (CURAJ), B.Sc. (CSJM University), Advanced PLFS Data Training (V.V. Giri National Labour Institute)
- **Experience** - Research Intern, IIT Delhi (HSS); Social Media Assistant, CURAJ; Country Ambassador - India, Dhaka University Research Society
- **Selected research and data projects** - PLFS labour indicators, women's labour trends, HCES household consumption, e-NAM digital agriculture, and exploratory Stata analyses for India, Uttar Pradesh and Rae Bareli
- **Dissertation** - Impact Assessment of Dvara KhetScore Model on Agricultural Credit Access among Farmers in Dudu and Phagi of Jaipur, Rajasthan (116 farm households; supervised by Dr. Jaya Kritika Ojha)
- **CV download** - [Academic CV](assets/cv/Mohd-Azeem-Academic-CV.pdf)
